Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how TheCapitalBox uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. These files allow websites to recognize your device and remember certain information about your visit, such as your preferences or actions.

Cookies help improve your browsing experience by making websites more efficient and personalized.

2. How We Use Cookies

TheCapitalBox uses cookies to enhance the functionality and performance of our website. These cookies may be used for the following purposes:

  • To ensure the website functions properly
  • To understand how visitors interact with our website
  • To improve website performance and usability
  • To remember user preferences and settings
  • To enhance the overall user experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. These cookies enable core features such as page navigation and access to secure areas of the website.

Without these cookies, the website may not function correctly.

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies collect information about how visitors use our website. This data helps us analyze traffic, understand user behavior, and improve website performance.

These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow the website to remember choices you make, such as language preferences or customized settings. These cookies provide a more personalized browsing experience.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies

Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain features and functionality of the website.

5. Updates to This Policy

TheCapitalBox may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or website functionality.

Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised Last Updated date.
